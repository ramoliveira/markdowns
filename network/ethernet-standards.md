# Ethernet Standards

* Is the most popular networking technology in the world
* Has many different types of Ethernet:
    * Speeds;
    * Cabling;
    * Connectors; and
    * Equipment.
* Modern Ethernet uses twisted pair copper or fiber

## IEEE Ethernet Standards

Is a standard maintained by the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE)
*802.3*: Committee related to ethernet networks

|IEEE Standard|Description|Media|Network Speed|
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
|1000BASE-T|Gigabit Ethernet|Copper|1 Gbit/s|
|10GBASE-T|10 Gigabit Ethernet|Copper|10 Gbit/s|
|1000BASE-SX|Gigabit Ethernet|Fiber|1 Gbit/s|

### Naming logic

Standard name is composed of speed, signal, and media. All contained in the standard name, i.e., **1000BASE-T**.

* The *number* is related to the network speed:
    * 1000 is commonly 1.000 Megabits per second (or 1 Gigabit/second).
    * 10G would be 10 Gigabits per second.

* The *BASE (baseband)* is related to the network signal:
    * Single frequency using the entire medium, i.e.: **BASE**.
    * Broadband uses many frequencies, sharing the medium, i.e.: **BROAD**.

* The *hyphen plus letter* is related to the media type:
    * **T** stands for twisted pair copper;
    * **F** stands for fiber;
    * **SX** would be short wavelength light.
